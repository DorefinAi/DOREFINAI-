What does Dorefin AI build?

Two AI products. Vartmaan AI collects reporter files for the assignment desk, matches footage, tags each story and turns it into an editable draft. Socia AI takes the approved story, writes a version for X, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, and schedules each one.

Can we use the products today?

Both products are in prerelease. We are building them in Jaipur with working newsroom teams. On the demo call we show you where each product stands and talk through what a pilot with your team could look like.

Do we have to replace our newsroom or publishing system?

No. Vartmaan AI reads from the channels your reporters already use: WhatsApp, Outlook, the newsroom FTP and RSS feeds. Socia AI publishes to your existing X, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube accounts.

Who decides what gets published?

Your team does. Vartmaan AI prepares drafts that your editors refine and approve. Socia AI works to the brand voice, guidelines and schedule your team sets.

Which teams is Dorefin for?

Vartmaan AI is for news channels, digital newsrooms, wire services and media houses. Socia AI is for digital media teams, social media managers, brand teams and content agencies.

What happens on the demo call?

You tell us what slows your team down. We show you the product closest to that problem, including where it would not fit your workflow.

Who is behind Dorefin AI?
