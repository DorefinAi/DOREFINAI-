Why Dorefin exists

Fast industries still run on slow tools.

Today

  • Reporter files arrive on WhatsApp, email and FTP, and someone has to piece them together before the desk can act.
  • One update is copied and pasted into five platforms by hand.
  • Editors spend their shift on matching, tagging and reformatting instead of editing.

With Dorefin

  • Every file lands in one intake, with footage matched and the story tagged by district and type.
  • Write once. Each platform gets its own format, tone and length, on a schedule.
  • Your team keeps the judgement calls. The repetitive steps run in the background.
