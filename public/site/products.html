Products

Two products, each built for one desk.

Vartmaan AI is for the assignment desk. Socia AI is for the team that publishes. Pick the one that matches the work slowing you down, or both.

Prerelease

Vartmaan AI

The assignment desk for news teams. Reporter files come in, editable story drafts go out.

  • Every source in one intakeReporter messages from WhatsApp and Outlook, footage on the newsroom FTP and Octopus RSS feeds arrive in one inbox.
  • Footage matched to the storyClips from the newsroom share are attached to the file they belong to.
  • Tagged by district and typeEach story comes in with its district and format, so the desk can sort and assign.
  • Drafts the desk can editRaw files become structured drafts. Editors edit, reject or approve them.

Socia AI

The publishing desk for digital teams. Approved stories go out to X, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube in your house style.

  • A version for each platformEach approved story is written for X, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube in the format that platform needs.
  • One queue to publish fromCheck, open and publish every post from one queue, with keyboard shortcuts.
  • Scheduled per channelEach version goes out at the time your team sets for that channel.
  • Desk analyticsReach, engagement and follower growth, plus what was received, approved, posted and rejected by district and category.

For digital media teams, social media managers, brand teams and content agencies.
