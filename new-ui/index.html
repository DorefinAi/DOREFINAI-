FAQ

Questions teams ask before the call.

Something else on your mind? Email arathore@dorefinai.com.

What does Dorefin AI build?

Two focused AI products. Vartmaan AI reads incoming wire feeds for newsrooms, surfaces what matters for the assignment desk and turns raw inputs into editable story drafts. Socia-L AI helps digital content teams write once, then adapts format, tone and length for each platform and schedules every post.

Can we use the products today?

Both products are in prerelease. We are building them in Jaipur with working newsroom teams. On the demo call we show you where each product stands and talk through what a pilot with your team could look like.

Do we have to replace our newsroom or publishing system?

No. Dorefin plugs into the systems your team already runs. There is no rip and replace and no new dashboard to learn.

Who decides what gets published?

Your team does. Vartmaan AI prepares drafts that your editors refine and approve. Socia-L AI works to the brand voice, guidelines and schedule your team sets.

Which teams is Dorefin for?

Vartmaan AI is for news channels, digital newsrooms, wire services and media houses. Socia-L AI is for digital media teams, social media managers, brand teams and content agencies.

What happens on the demo call?

You tell us what slows your team down. We show you the product closest to that problem and tell you honestly where Dorefin fits and where it does not. No sales pitch, just a real conversation.

Who is behind Dorefin AI?

Dorefin AI Technologies Pvt Ltd is registered in Rajasthan and based in Jaipur. It was founded in 2026 by Aditya Rathore, who has built SaaS platforms across supply chain, fintech and DeFi.
